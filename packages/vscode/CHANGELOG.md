# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Grammar synced with `@mmmnt/core` 0.6: highlights `projection`, `query`,
  `rule`, `term`, `external`, `orchestration`, `state`, `gateway` and their
  clauses. The grammar had not been synced since 0.1.0.

## 0.1.1

- Listing meta: mmmnt brand mark as the extension icon and gallery banner

## 0.1.0

- Initial release: TextMate grammar for `.moment` files (generated from the
  Langium grammar in `@mmmnt/core`), including the saga transition event
  mapping syntax (`states A -> B on Event`)
- Language configuration: `//` and `/* */` comments, bracket matching,
  auto-closing pairs
