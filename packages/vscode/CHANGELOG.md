## 0.1.0

- Initial release: TextMate grammar for `.moment` files (generated from the
  Langium grammar in `@mmmnt/core`), including the saga transition event
  mapping syntax (`states A -> B on Event`)
- Language configuration: `//` and `/* */` comments, bracket matching,
  auto-closing pairs
